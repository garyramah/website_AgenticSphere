The whole new environment where agents act — the sum of every agent’s reachable world: the tools it can touch, the data it can read, the systems it can move, the chains it can compose. This is the territory. The authority map charts it. This page names it — and sets the standard for mapping it.

What the realm contains

The inhabitants

Every agent that acts

Assistants, daemons, fleets, services — everything that acts on someone’s behalf, with or without them watching. The realm is defined by action, not by vendor.

The edges

Where authority ends

Every sphere has an edge: the boundary of what an agent may reach. Where spheres overlap, authority composes — and that composition is where the realm gets interesting, and dangerous.

The weather

Drift, moving through

Overreach patterns, shared defaults, quiet exceedances — weather fronts that cross the whole realm at once. The Lighthouse watches this weather and warns of it. The Sphere is the sky it moves through.

The map and the territory

The authority map charts who may reach what, and how grants compose across hops. The sphere is the territory that map covers. Neither replaces the other: a map without a territory is a diagram, and a territory without a map is a wilderness. The atlas project is the work of keeping the two honest to each other — mapping what is actually reachable, not what was intended to be.
